node 1 about

I’ll teach you to think, not to cram

I hold a master’s degree in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science. I’ve been programming since I was 13 and have loved logic puzzles since early childhood. I used to work at Yandex.

I write and teach algorithms courses at Shortcut school: a core course for junior and middle developers, and an advanced one.

I believe any self-respecting engineer should understand how the things they work with are built. And I believe algorithmic problems are not just for interview prep: they broaden your engineering horizons and intuition, teach you to reason, notice details and spot the tricky parts of a problem in advance, and make you sharper overall.

In my Telegram channel «Коровкина про алгоритмы» I walk through problems from real interviews, share tips for preparing, and explain hard topics in plain words.

  • 01
    Why, not just howI explain why a solution works. Then you can rebuild it in an interview instead of trying to remember it by heart.
  • 02
    Problems from real interviewsPractice is built on problems that candidates actually get at big tech companies.
  • 03
    Mistakes in advanceI go through typical mistakes before you make them in front of an interviewer.

Learning algorithms means learning to think like an engineer.
